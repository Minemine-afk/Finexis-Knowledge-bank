Coverage Bench is a calculator, not advice, and not a product summary. The panel is real and every record links to the insurer page it came from, but the premiums, payout multiples and illustrated values in here are estimates — read the marker before you quote a number to anyone.

The hospital arithmetic models the standard shape of an Integrated Shield Plan — pro-ration, deductible, co-insurance, annual limit — which real policies vary in dozens of ways this tool does not read: waiting periods, pre-existing exclusions, panel restrictions, sub-limits per procedure, and how a claim is actually assessed. Riders sold from 1 April 2026 no longer cover the deductible, and the model reflects that.

Savings and investment figures are illustrations at the regulatory rates — participating funds at 3.00% and 4.25%, investment-linked at 4% and 8% — not projections, and not guarantees. Check anything that matters against the product summary, the benefit illustration and the insurer's own quote.
